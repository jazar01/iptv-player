Sign in

The admin password is $AdminPassword in scripts\deploy.local.ps1 on the computer the app is installed from.

Each TV backs itself up to this Raspberry Pi within a minute of a change. A TV that loses its saved data (a reinstall) offers to restore its backup when it starts.

What a new TV starts from: after "Set up as a new TV", it asks only for its name. TVs already set up aren't changed.

      Restart or shut down

      The TVs play straight from the provider while the Pi is off: no live buffer, Dolby conversion or backups until it's back. A restart takes about 15 seconds. After a shut down, the Pi stays off until its power is unplugged and plugged in again.
